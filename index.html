About Vikalpa Chitra
✦

Visual stories from a slower way of looking.

Vikalpa Chitra is a personal art archive built around the idea that a painting can be experienced beyond its frame. The physical artwork remains the centre, while its digital page preserves the context, process and voice behind it.

Watercolour, graphite, charcoal, acrylic and texture-led studies form the evolving collection.
