The artist
✦

Visual stories from a slower way of looking.

Vikalpa Chitra is the artist identity of Paila Vamsi Kiran. The practice moves between watercolour, graphite, charcoal, acrylic and texture-led studies, with a focus on atmosphere, memory and everyday observation.

The digital gallery is an extension of the physical work. Every artwork can carry a story, a voice recording and a permanent QR destination so the experience continues beyond the frame.
